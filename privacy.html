    Privacy policy

    What we collect

    We collect only what you enter in the apply form: your name, email address, year of study, team size and any accessibility or dietary needs you choose to share.

    How we use it

    We use your details to run HackTrent: to plan food and space, match teams and email you about the event. We don’t sell the details you give us or share them with anyone outside the organizing team.

    Fonts

    This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. We send nothing else.

    Your choices

    You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email hello@hacktrent.example and we’ll reply within two working days.
