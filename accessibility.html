    Accessibility

    We want everyone to be able to use this website and take part in HackTrent.

    What we’ve done

    • You can reach every link, button and form field with the keyboard. Use “Skip to main content” at the top of each page to jump past the menu.
    • Text and background colours meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, and text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.
    • Pages use headings, landmarks, labelled form fields and descriptive image text so screen readers can navigate them.
    • Animation is limited and turns off if your device asks for reduced motion.

    What we’re still checking

    We’ve tested with automated tools and the keyboard. We’re still testing with a wider range of screen readers.

    Tell us about a problem

    If something doesn’t work for you, email hello@hacktrent.example. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and what happened. You can also ask for access needs at the event when you apply.
