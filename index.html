    About us

    HackTrent is a student-run hackathon. Over one weekend, teams turn an idea into a working project and show it to everyone on Sunday. You don’t need to be a computer science student, and you don’t need to have entered a hackathon before.

    Mentors are on hand all weekend, workshops cover the basics, and there is always food. Last year’s event ran on November 8 and 9, 2025.
